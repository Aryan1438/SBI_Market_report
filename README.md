# State Bank of India Market Research

This repository contains my research work on **State Bank of India (SBI)**. The work was prepared as part of my research internship and combines a written initiation report with a supporting financial model.

## What I Worked On

The research focuses on understanding SBI as a listed banking company and evaluating its market and financial position. My work included:

- Reviewing SBI's business profile, banking operations, and position in the Indian banking sector.
- Studying the factors that can influence SBI's share-price performance and investor perception.
- Collecting and organizing relevant company and market information for analysis.
- Preparing a financial model to structure historical information and support the evaluation of SBI's performance.
- Connecting the qualitative findings from the research report with the quantitative analysis in the model.
- Documenting the initial research scope, approach, assumptions, and areas for further analysis.

## Research Materials

- **[SBI Initiation Report](SBI_Initiation_Report.docx)**: written research covering the company, sector context, investment-relevant observations, and the planned analytical approach.
- **[SBI Financial Model](SBI_Financial_Model.xlsx)**: spreadsheet-based analysis used to organize financial data and support the research process.

## Purpose of This Repository

This is an academic and professional research record of the work completed during my internship. It is intended to show the research process and analysis performed on SBI, not to provide investment advice or a trading recommendation.

## Scope and Limitations

The analysis reflects the information, assumptions, and research scope available when the report and model were prepared. Financial markets and company fundamentals change over time, so the material should be read in that context and independently verified before being used for any investment decision.
